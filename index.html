The Story That Started It All

The Lost Sheep
The Shearer's Tool
The Shepherd's Garment

A cynical 17-year-old British-Nigerian podcaster posts an ad inviting Jesus to an interview. When Jesus actually shows up in a leather jacket, Ebun is yanked through time and space—from war-torn Syria to the South African veldt to an otherworldly shearing barn—where he's forced to confront the spiritual captivity he didn't know he was in.

This is the gospel—unfiltered, multicultural, and impossible to ignore.
